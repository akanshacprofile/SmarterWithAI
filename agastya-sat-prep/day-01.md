## MATH LESSON (15 min)

### Topic: Solving Quadratics by Factoring

A quadratic has the form: **ax² + bx + c = 0**

**Method:** Find two numbers that multiply to `a·c` and add to `b`. Split the middle term, factor by grouping.

#### Worked example 1
Solve `x² + 5x + 6 = 0`
- Need two numbers: multiply to 6, add to 5 → **2 and 3**
- Factor: `(x + 2)(x + 3) = 0`
- Solutions: **x = −2, x = −3**

#### Worked example 2
Solve `2x² − 7x + 3 = 0`
- a·c = 2·3 = 6. Need two numbers: multiply to 6, add to −7 → **−6 and −1**
- Rewrite: `2x² − 6x − x + 3 = 0`
- Group: `2x(x − 3) − 1(x − 3) = 0` → `(2x − 1)(x − 3) = 0`
- Solutions: **x = 1/2, x = 3**

#### Speed tip
If `a = 1`, just find two numbers that multiply to `c` and add to `b`. Don't overthink.

## ENGLISH LESSON (15 min)

### Topic: Comma Rules (5 rules tested on SAT)

#### Rule 1 — Comma before FANBOYS joining two independent clauses
`I went home, and I slept.` ✓ (both sides are full sentences)
`I went home and slept.` ✓ (NO comma — "slept" isn't a full clause)

#### Rule 2 — Comma after introductory phrase
`After the game, we ate pizza.` ✓
`In 1985, the company was founded.` ✓

#### Rule 3 — Commas around non-essential information
`My brother, who lives in Texas, called me.` ✓ (info is extra, not needed to ID brother)
`The boy who broke the window apologized.` ✓ (NO commas — info IS needed to ID the boy)

#### Rule 4 — Commas in a list of 3+
`I bought apples, bread, and milk.` ✓

#### Rule 5 — NO comma between subject and verb
`The girl in the red dress, was singing.` ✗
`The girl in the red dress was singing.` ✓

#### Speed tip
If you see a comma, ask: which of the 5 rules does it satisfy? If none → wrong.
