# Agastya's 10-Day SAT Speed & Accuracy Program

**Current score:** 1300 (R&W 630 / Math 670 inferred)
**Goal:** Raise weakest domains + train speed.

## Daily Routine (35 min)

```
0:00 – 0:15   MATH lesson (read teaching section)
0:15 – 0:30   MATH practice: 10 questions, timer on
0:30 – 0:35   Mark wrong + jot WHY in tracker
0:35 – 0:50   ENGLISH lesson (read teaching section)
0:50 – 1:05   ENGLISH practice: 12 questions, timer on
1:05 – 1:10   Mark wrong + jot WHY in tracker
```

If 70 min is too much in one block, split: math in morning, english in evening.

## Difficulty Progression

| Days | Difficulty | Pace target |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Foundation (easy SAT level) | Untimed → 100 sec/Q math, 85 sec/Q English |
| 4–6 | Medium (mid SAT level) | 95 sec/Q math, 80 sec/Q English |
| 7–9 | Hard (top SAT level) | 90 sec/Q math, 75 sec/Q English |
| 10 | Full timed mock | Strict SAT pace |

## Question Distribution Per Day

**Math (10 Qs):** 4 Advanced Math · 3 Problem-Solving & Data Analysis · 2 Algebra · 1 Geometry/Trig
**English (12 Qs):** 5 Standard English Conventions · 4 Craft & Structure · 2 Expression of Ideas · 1 Information & Ideas

## Files

## How to Track Improvement

Each day fill in the row in `tracker.md`:
- Correct / total
- Average seconds per question (total time ÷ questions)
- One sentence on what was hardest

By Day 10, compare Day 1 vs Day 10:
- **Accuracy goal:** +20% on weakest domain
- **Speed goal:** −15 sec/Q on both sections

## Rules of the Road

1. **Two-pass:** skip any Q taking >90 sec math / >75 sec English. Return at end.
2. **Wrong-answer log:** every miss gets one line: *what I picked / what was right / why I missed it.*
3. **The 90-second rule:** if stuck, guess and move. Speed comes from cutting losses.
4. **No calculator dependency:** SAT allows calculator on all math, but try the first attempt by hand to build number sense.

Start with `day-01.md`.
