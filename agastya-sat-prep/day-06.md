## MATH LESSON (15 min)

### Topic: Polynomials — Operations, Zeros, End Behavior

#### Multiplying polynomials (FOIL and beyond)
`(x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 5x + 6`
`(x − 1)(x² + 3x + 2)` → distribute every term:
= x³ + 3x² + 2x − x² − 3x − 2 = **x³ + 2x² − x − 2**

#### Zeros of a polynomial
The zeros are the x-values where y = 0. They come from the factors.
- `y = (x − 2)(x + 5)(x − 1)` has zeros at x = 2, x = −5, x = 1.

#### Connecting zeros to factors
If r is a zero, then (x − r) is a factor.
- Polynomial with zeros at 1, 2, 3 → `(x−1)(x−2)(x−3)`

#### Worked example 1
Find zeros of `f(x) = x³ − 4x`.
- Factor: `x(x² − 4) = x(x−2)(x+2)`
- Zeros: **x = 0, 2, −2**

#### Worked example 2
What is the remainder when `x³ + 2x − 5` is divided by `(x − 1)`?
- **Remainder Theorem:** remainder = f(1) = 1 + 2 − 5 = **−2**

#### Speed tip
Remainder Theorem: to find remainder of P(x) ÷ (x − a), just plug a into P(x). No long division needed.

## ENGLISH LESSON (15 min)

### Topic: Verb Tense & Verb Form

#### Rule 1 — Tense consistency
All verbs in a sentence should match in time unless logic demands a shift.
- ❌ "She walked to the store and **buys** milk."
- ✓ "She walked to the store and **bought** milk."

#### Rule 2 — Past perfect for "earlier past"
Use **had + past participle** when an action happened BEFORE another past action.
- ✓ "By the time I arrived, the meeting **had ended**."

#### Rule 3 — Present perfect for ongoing past
Use **has/have + past participle** for actions started in past and continuing now, or at unspecified past.
- ✓ "She **has lived** here since 2010."

#### Rule 4 — Subjunctive (after "if" hypothetical or "wish")
- ✓ "If I **were** rich, I would travel." (NOT "if I was")
- ✓ "I wish it **were** Friday."

#### Rule 5 — Gerunds vs. infinitives
Some verbs take **gerund** (-ing): enjoy, finish, avoid, suggest.
Some take **infinitive** (to + verb): want, decide, hope, agree.
- ✓ "She enjoys **reading**." (NOT "to read")
- ✓ "He decided **to leave**." (NOT "leaving")

#### Rule 6 — "Have done" vs "have did"
The third form (past participle) follows "have," never the past tense.
- ❌ "She has **wrote** a book."
- ✓ "She has **written** a book."

#### Speed tip
Time clue words signal tense: *yesterday* → past; *since/for years* → present perfect; *by the time* → past perfect.
