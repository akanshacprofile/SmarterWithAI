## MATH MOCK (10 Qs, 15 min)

**Q1 (Advanced Math).** What are the solutions to `3x² − 11x − 4 = 0`?
A) x = 4, x = −1/3   B) x = −4, x = 1/3   C) x = 4, x = 1/3   D) x = −4, x = −1/3

**Q2 (Advanced Math).** The parabola `y = 2(x + 1)² − 8` has its minimum value at:
A) −8   B) −1   C) 1   D) 8

**Q3 (Advanced Math).** A bacterial population doubles every 3 hours. If there are 200 bacteria now, how many in 9 hours?
A) 600   B) 800   C) 1,200   D) 1,600

**Q4 (Advanced Math).** Simplify: `(x² − 9) / (x² + 6x + 9)`
A) (x − 3)/(x + 3)   B) (x + 3)/(x − 3)   C) (x − 3)/(x − 3)   D) x − 3

**Q5 (Problem-Solving & Data Analysis).** A class of 20 students has a mean test score of 75. If a new student joins with a score of 96, what is the new mean?
A) 75   B) 76   C) 77   D) 78

**Q6 (Problem-Solving & Data Analysis).** A poll of 800 voters with margin of error ±3.5% finds 47% support a measure. Which conclusion is supported?
A) The measure will pass   B) Between 43.5% and 50.5% support, within margin of error   C) Exactly 47% support   D) Less than 43.5% support

**Q7 (Problem-Solving & Data Analysis).** Two-way table:
|        | Likes Coffee | Doesn't | Total |
|--------|--------------|---------|-------|
| Adults | 80           | 20      | 100   |
| Teens  | 30           | 70      | 100   |
| Total  | 110          | 90      | 200   |

What fraction of coffee-lovers are teens?
A) 3/11   B) 3/10   C) 11/20   D) 7/10

**Q8 (Algebra).** A line passes through (0, 7) and (4, 15). What is its slope?
A) 1   B) 2   C) 4   D) 8

**Q9 (Algebra).** Solve: `5x − 3 ≥ 2x + 9`
A) x ≥ 2   B) x ≥ 4   C) x ≤ 4   D) x ≥ 6

**Q10 (Geometry).** A triangle has sides 9, 12, and 15. Is it a right triangle?
A) Yes — 9² + 12² = 15²   B) No — sides don't form right angle   C) Cannot determine   D) Yes — by 30-60-90

## ENGLISH MOCK (12 Qs, 15 min)

**Q1 (SEC).** "Before submitting the final draft ____ Maya proofread every paragraph."
A) ,   B) ;   C) :   D) (no punctuation)

**Q2 (SEC).** "The committee had three concerns ____ cost, timing, and feasibility."
A) ,   B) ;   C) :   D) and

**Q3 (SEC).** "Each of the proposals ____ been carefully evaluated."
A) have   B) has   C) is   D) were

**Q4 (SEC).** "The award was given to my colleague and ____."
A) I   B) me   C) myself   D) my

**Q5 (SEC).** "She is not only a talented writer but also ____."
A) plays piano   B) a skilled musician   C) was a teacher   D) wins awards

**Q6 (Craft & Structure — vocab).** "The senator's _______ to compromise on every issue eventually cost her the support of her base."
A) refusal   B) willingness   C) hesitation   D) eagerness

**Q7 (Craft & Structure — vocab).** "Though the artist was once dismissed as _______, her later work is now considered visionary."
A) brilliant   B) derivative   C) influential   D) profound

**Q8 (Craft & Structure — rhetorical choice).** Passage: "The river is not just dying. It is being killed — by every factory, every farm, every dollar that values profit over life."
The author's use of "killed" rather than "dying" most directly serves to:
A) provide a scientific term   B) shift blame to specific agents   C) lighten the tone   D) describe the river's appearance

**Q9 (Craft & Structure — text structure).** Passage: "First, the company posted record profits. Then, executives received massive bonuses. Finally, the company laid off 5,000 workers."
The structure most strongly suggests the author's purpose is to:
A) describe events neutrally   B) imply moral criticism through sequence   C) celebrate the company   D) compare two industries

**Q10 (Expression of Ideas — transition).** "The drug significantly reduced symptoms in trials. ____, some patients reported severe side effects."
A) Moreover   B) However   C) Therefore   D) Specifically

**Q11 (Expression of Ideas — rhetorical synthesis).** Notes:
- The Great Pacific Garbage Patch is twice the size of Texas.
- It's composed mostly of microplastics, not large debris.
- Marine animals ingest microplastics, which enter the food chain.
- Cleaning up the patch requires international cooperation.

The student wants to **highlight the human health implications**. Which sentence does this best?
A) The Great Pacific Garbage Patch is twice the size of Texas.
B) Because marine animals ingest microplastics, which enter the food chain, the patch poses a direct risk to human health.
C) It's mostly composed of microplastics, not large debris.
D) Cleaning up the patch requires international cooperation.

**Q12 (Information & Ideas).** Passage: "Forests once covered most of Europe. Today, less than 40% of the continent is forested, with most remaining forests heavily managed for timber rather than wild."
The main idea most likely being developed is:
A) Europe is large   B) Europe has lost most of its wild forest over time   C) Timber is valuable   D) Forests grow back quickly

3. **D** — 200 × 2³ = 1600
4. **A** — (x−3)(x+3) / (x+3)² = (x−3)/(x+3)

6. **B** — 47 ± 3.5 = 43.5 to 50.5
7. **A** — 30/110 = 3/11
8. **B** — (15−7)/(4−0) = 2
9. **B** — 3x ≥ 12 → x ≥ 4
10. **A** — 81 + 144 = 225; 15² = 225 → right triangle

### English
1. **A** — comma after introductory phrase
2. **C** — colon after IC introduces list
3. **B** — "each" is singular
4. **B** — object of "to" → me
5. **B** — parallel noun phrase ("a talented writer" / "a skilled musician")
6. **A** — "cost her support" = negative consequence; "refusal to compromise" makes that sense
7. **B** — once dismissed, NOW visionary → originally was seen as unoriginal → derivative
8. **B** — "killed" assigns agency to specific actors
9. **B** — sequence implies criticism (profits → bonuses → layoffs)
10. **B** — promise BUT side effects = contrast
11. **B** — connects food chain to human health
12. **B** — lost most wild forest = main historical claim
